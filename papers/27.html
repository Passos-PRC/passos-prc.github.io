Second-generation capsule endoscopy for the detection of colorectal polyps: An updated systematic review and comparative meta-analysis of prospective studies

Câmara CF, Passos PRC, Cezar ECL, Filho JNL, Oliveira RMA, et al.

Summary

A systematic review and Bayesian comparative meta-analysis of second-generation colon capsule endoscopy for detecting colorectal polyps, with colonoscopy as the reference. Twelve prospective studies with 4,316 patients were analyzed, and a simulated cohort of 1,000 patients was used for decision curve analysis.

Key findings

  • Sensitivity and specificity were 0.89 and 0.94 for polyps of 6 mm or more, and 0.91 and 0.98 for polyps of 10 mm or more.
  • Performance was better in patients with a positive fecal immunochemical test.
  • In the simulation, capsule endoscopy had greater net benefit than colonoscopy for everyone from a 20% pre-test probability of having a polyp.
  • It is a safe, accurate alternative for patients who refuse or cannot have colonoscopy, but cannot treat lesions and has no mortality evidence.
