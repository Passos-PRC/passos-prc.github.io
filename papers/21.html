Hepatic Events Prevention by Antihyperglycemic Therapies and Intervention Comparisons in Type 2 Diabetes: The HEPATIC-T2DM Network Meta-analysis

Passos PRC, Motta R, Oliveira Costa Filho V, Noronha MM, Venâncio RC, et al.

Summary

The HEPATIC-T2DM network meta-analysis compares glucose-lowering drug classes for major adverse liver outcomes in adults with type 2 diabetes. A three-level Bayesian model pooled 46 observational studies with more than 7.1 million patients.

Key findings

  • Thiazolidinediones had the lowest association with hepatocellular carcinoma, lower than DPP-4 inhibitors (HR 0.50), GLP-1 receptor agonists (HR 0.72), insulin (HR 0.20) and sulfonylureas (HR 0.69).
  • GLP-1 receptor agonists had the lowest hazard of decompensation, variceal bleeding and hepatic encephalopathy.
  • SGLT2 inhibitors had the lowest association with cirrhosis and with liver-related mortality.
  • All studies were observational, so causal inference is not possible; randomized trials are needed.
