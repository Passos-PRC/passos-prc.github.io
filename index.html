Medical Researcher

Pedro Robson Costa Passos

I work on the immune-mediated side of the liver and gut: liver transplantation, autoimmune liver disease and inflammatory bowel disease. My tools are clinical epidemiology, evidence synthesis and prognostic models, mostly in R.
